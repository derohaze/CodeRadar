Review work scatters fast: a linter here, a security scanner there, AI comments in the PR, coverage in another tab, and nobody sure which signal to trust.

CodeRadar is designed to bring that work into one review surface with one rule — authority is the code, not the model.

## What CodeRadar brings together

CodeRadar focuses on the responsibilities that keep review trustworthy:

- Source scope: files, folders, diffs, changed lines.
- Findings with quoted evidence at real lines.
- Dropped candidates with rejection reasons.
- Coverage: files, blocks, paths, elapsed time.
- Fix prompts ready for your coding agent.
- Honest states: complete, partial, degraded, failed.

The direction is not another dashboard for passive viewing. The goal is to make CodeRadar the place where review work is understood, decided, and finished.

## One bar for every signal

| Review question | CodeRadar focus |
| --- | --- |
| Is this finding real? | Evidence gate: quote must exist in the named file. |
| What did the review skip? | Limitations panel with explicit states. |
| What was rejected? | Dropped candidates with reasons. |
| What do I fix first? | Severity plus confidence ordering. |
| How do I fix it? | Concrete fix plus copy-paste agent prompt. |

## The CodeRadar outcome

CodeRadar exists to make review easier to trust as codebases grow. When every claim carries its evidence, the team can fix faster, argue less, and ship with more confidence.
